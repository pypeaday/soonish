Eisenhower Matrix

The heart of QuadTask - organize tasks using the proven Eisenhower Method:

Urgent & Important (Do First)
Not Urgent but Important (Schedule)
Urgent but Not Important (Delegate)
Neither Urgent nor Important (Eliminate)

Perfect for strategic planning and prioritization.

Hi! I'm passionate about creating tools that help people be more productive and organized.
